Editorial illustration, 16:9 landscape.

SCENE — One single scene, no panels, no frames. A phone held in one hand, drawn LARGE:
it sits across the middle of the picture, tilted a little, seen from slightly above and
slightly to the side, and it takes up about half the width and two-thirds of the height of
the image. The hand holding it belongs to a young woman of about seventeen; we see her
hand, her wrist and her forearm coming up from the bottom right corner, and nothing else
of her — her face is not in the picture at all.

WHAT IS ON THE SCREEN — The phone screen shows ONE THING ONLY: a photograph the group just
took of themselves, drawn in the same hand-drawn illustration style as the rest of the
picture but very slightly cooler and softer, so it reads as a picture inside a picture.
There is NO interface of any kind on that screen: no buttons, no icons, no menu bars, no
shutter button, no time, no battery, no rounded panels, no text — only the photograph,
filling the screen edge to edge.
IN THAT PHOTOGRAPH: four friends aged about sixteen to twenty-two, crowded shoulder to
shoulder, mid-laugh.
  • One of them, a young man of about eighteen, is laughing with his mouth wide open and
    WEARS FIXED METAL BRACES that are easy to see: five or six small plain square
    silver-grey brackets on his upper front teeth, one per tooth, joined by a single thin
    metal wire straight across them. Drawn simply — small squares and one line, never a
    photographic close-up, never shiny metal;
  • one is laughing with her head thrown back;
  • one has an open easy grin;
  • AND THE GIRL IN THE MIDDLE — the same young woman whose hand holds the phone — IS THE
    ONLY ONE WHOSE MOUTH IS CLOSED. Her lips are pressed gently together into a small
    closed-mouth smile while everyone around her laughs out loud. Her eyes are still
    smiling and her cheeks are still raised; she is enjoying herself. She is NOT sad, NOT
    embarrassed, NOT frowning, NOT crying, and she is not turned away from the others.
    The single readable fact of this picture is that everyone else's teeth show and hers
    do not.
Keep the faces inside the photograph large enough to read clearly at a glance.
The phone screen is the ONLY rectangle in this picture. It is a phone, not a comic
panel: there are no other frames, borders or boxes anywhere in the image.

THE THUMB — Her thumb rests near the lower edge of the screen without touching it,
hovering, as if she has been looking at this photo for a moment longer than she needed to.
The thumb points at nothing in particular; there is no button under it.

AROUND THE PHONE — The real world shows only at the edges of the picture, out of focus in
the sense that it is drawn more loosely and with less detail than the phone: the pale wood
of a small table along the bottom, two sealed drink cups with completely plain surfaces,
a bunched paper napkin, a set of keys, and — coming in from the top left corner — the
forearm and shoulder of one friend still laughing, cropped by the edge of the picture so
we never see that face. Warm late-afternoon light falls from the upper left across the
table. Leave generous areas of bare pale paper around the phone; do not fill the corners.

MOVEMENT — Beside the cropped laughing friend at the top left, put THREE OR FOUR SHORT
PARALLEL WHITE HAND-DRAWN STROKES at the same slant, solid where they begin and thinning
to dry flecks at the far end, standing for the laughter still going on around her. Small
and quiet; never one long continuous ribbon, never curling or looping, never smoke or
steam, and no cartoon symbols of any kind.

THE PHONE ITSELF — Drawn simply and flatly: a plain rounded rectangle in soft warm grey
with a thin hand-drawn outline, a single small camera circle on the back if any of the
back shows. No brand mark, no logo, no engraved details, no glossy reflection, no glare
across the glass, no highlight sweep. The screen is not shiny.
THE ACCENT BLUE goes in exactly three places: the phone's case, one drink cup's lid, and
the shirt of the friend at the top left.

PEOPLE — Simplified illustrative people, drawn as East Asian teenagers and young adults:
minimal features (small dot or short-line eyes, a simple line mouth, a small nose),
natural realistic head-to-body proportions, believable ages. Not photorealistic, not
faceless, no oversized heads, no noodle limbs, and NO large glossy anime eyes. Everyone in
this picture is between about fifteen and twenty-five years old — school and university
age. There are no middle-aged adults, no parents, no children and no elderly people
anywhere in the image.
NOBODY LOOKS AT THE VIEWER. Every face is turned toward something inside the picture.

CLOTHING — clothes are NEVER flat single-tone shapes. Model each garment with two or three
tones of its own colour: soft coloured-pencil shading in the folds, along the sleeves,
under the collar and at the hem, with the real details drawn — collar, cuffs, sleeve seam,
the ribbing at a cuff, the knit of a jumper, the drawstring of a hoodie. Everyone wears a
clearly different colour; no two people wear the same hue.

TEETH — Every tooth in this picture is clean and near-white: no yellow, no beige, no decay,
no dark spots, no missing teeth, no red or swollen gums, no blood. Draw teeth simply and
illustratively — a soft row, never a photorealistic mouth interior, never individually
outlined in heavy black.

STYLE — Contemporary printed-magazine editorial illustration, light and airy. Linework in
warm dark brown or soft charcoal, NEVER pure black: thin, hand-drawn, the weight varies,
strokes taper and sometimes break. Colour applied like soft coloured pencil and light
marker, flat fills with two or three tones per hue and no smooth decorative gradients,
with plenty of the pale paper left showing through — large parts of the ground, the walls
and the air stay almost bare paper. Colour edges a little loose, not always meeting the
line. A fine even paper grain over the whole image. Nothing is glossy, nothing is rendered
in 3D, nothing carries a photographic highlight.

COLOUR — Light, muted and gentle: pale cream and off-white as the ground, warm sand, dusty
sage, soft warm grey, pale butter yellow, muted clay, soft terracotta. Desaturated
throughout — nothing heavy, no neon, no dark heavy shadows. Colour throughout, never
greyscale.
The ONE accent colour of this picture is a muted mid blue, used sparingly and only where
the scene description names it. Everything else stays in the warm neutral family. Do NOT
wash the whole picture in blue, do NOT use a bright or electric blue, and do NOT let blue
touch skin, faces or the sky.

CRITICAL — NO WRITING ANYWHERE IN THE IMAGE. No text, letters, words, numbers, logos,
signage, captions, labels, watermarks, price tags, menus, screen interfaces, app icons,
buttons or numeral clock faces, in any language. Cups, packaging, bags, shopfronts,
posters, clothing and every screen carry no printing of any kind. Where writing would
normally appear, leave the surface plain.

AVOID — greyscale; photorealism; 3D rendering; glossy digital painting; anime or manga
styling with large glossy eyes; thick uniform black outlines; chrome, metallic or
iridescent gradients; any recognisable branded, franchise or licensed character, mascot,
figurine or logo; any before-and-after or good-versus-bad comparison; a smile chart or a
row of graded smiles; any panel borders, gutters, frames or comic-strip divisions; speech
balloons, thought bubbles, arrows, circles, spotlights or labels pointing at anyone's
mouth; a dental clinic treatment room; a reclining dental chair; an overhead operating
lamp; a drill, syringe, needle, probe or tray of instruments; dental models, plaster casts
or appliances lying on a table; hands inside a mouth; a photorealistic open mouth or mouth
interior; blood, swelling, pain, fear or embarrassment; anyone crying, sulking or covering
their face in shame; anyone looking at the viewer; middle-aged or elderly people.
