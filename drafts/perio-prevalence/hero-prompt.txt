Editorial illustration, 16:9 landscape.

STYLE — READ THIS FIRST; IT MATTERS MORE THAN ANY OTHER SECTION.
Contemporary printed-magazine editorial illustration, hand-made throughout. Linework in
warm dark brown or soft charcoal, NEVER pure black: thin, hand-drawn, the weight visibly
varies along a stroke, strokes taper and sometimes break and run dry at the end — never an
even mechanical vector line. Colour applied like soft coloured pencil and light marker;
colour edges a little loose and not always meeting the line. Flat fills with two or three
tones per hue — no smooth decorative gradients, no airbrush, no glow. A fine even paper
grain over every surface of the whole image.
SKIN IS THE ONE EXCEPTION to the two-or-three-tones rule: each face and each hand is ONE
single flat tone, with no modelling, no blush, no shading, no cheekbones and no jawline.
On a face, draw ONLY these six things: the outline of the head, the eyes, the eyebrows,
the nose, the mouth and the ears. Nothing else at all — no wrinkles, no lines beside the
nose, no lines under the eyes, no cheek shadow, no neck shading.
HAIR IS ALWAYS A WARM DARK BROWN — #3f3330, its shadowed side #2e2523, its lit edge
#4d403c. NEVER grey, silver, white, ash, steel or blue-grey hair on anyone.
EVERY PERSON IN THIS PICTURE IS DRAWN BY THE SAME HAND, at the same level of simplicity:
nobody is rendered in more detail than anybody else, and nobody is drawn in a different
style from the rest.

STRUCTURE — ONE single illustration divided by THIN HAND-DRAWN LINES into a large middle
panel and four small side panels. NO ARROWS ANYWHERE.
  • THE MIDDLE PANEL fills the central 56% of the width and the full height. It is the main
    picture and everything in it is drawn large.
  • TWO SMALL PANELS are stacked in the left 22% of the width, and TWO SMALL PANELS are
    stacked in the right 22%. Each small panel holds ONE simple scene with only TWO people
    in it — never more — because these panels are small and must stay readable.

THE MIDDLE PANEL — A BRIGHT, ORDINARY DAYTIME OPERATIONS ROOM, seen wide. Pale walls, a
large window on the left letting in clean daylight, a pale wooden floor, low desks with
keyboards and paper folders, a potted plant, a wheeled chair, and a round wall clock whose
face is completely blank. This is NOT a dark high-tech command centre: it is a light, calm,
ordinary working room.
  • THE BIG SCREEN IS THE FOCAL POINT: one large, thin, PALE TRANSLUCENT screen standing
    against the back wall, filling most of the upper two thirds of this panel. It is pale
    and airy, never a glowing dark monitor.
  • ON THAT SCREEN, IN THE CENTRE, THERE IS ONE ISLAND — the island of Taiwan, drawn as a
    single simplified silhouette. USE THE ATTACHED REFERENCE IMAGE FOR ITS SHAPE AND
    PROPORTIONS ONLY, NOT FOR ITS STYLE: the island is clearly TALLER THAN IT IS WIDE, about
    twice as tall as it is wide; its western edge is smooth and gently curved, its eastern
    edge is straighter and steeper; the northern end is blunt and the southern end narrows
    to a soft point. Draw the island TALL — it takes up at least two thirds of the height of
    the screen. It is the single most important object in the whole picture and it must
    still read as an island when the picture is shrunk to the size of a postage stamp.
  • THE ISLAND IS SHADED LIKE A HEAT MAP, in three flat steps of ONE colour: the palest
    areas are a light tint of #317d78, the middle areas are #317d78 itself, and the few
    deepest patches are #2a6d69. The patches are soft irregular shapes that meet edge to
    edge. DO NOT draw internal borders, administrative regions, county outlines, grid lines
    or a dotted coastline — just soft shaded areas inside one clean island outline.
  • FOUR SMALL AMBER DOTS (#c28229) sit on the island, spread from north to south.
  • FOUR NUMBERS float on the screen beside the island, two on its left and two on its
    right, each joined to the EDGE of the island by one thin short leader line. The four
    numbers are exactly: 78.7% and 24.7% on one side, 9.3% and 6.9% on the other. They are
    plain, upright, evenly spaced sans-serif digits followed by a percent sign, and nothing
    else — no words, no place names, no labels beside them.
  • TWO SMALL AMBER WARNING TRIANGLES (#c28229), each no wider than a finger, sit near the
    island — each one a plain triangle with a single short vertical exclamation stroke and a
    dot below it inside the triangle.
  • CHARTS SIT ROUND THE ISLAND ON THE SAME SCREEN, small and simple, drawn as clean flat
    shapes: a column chart of FIVE bars of clearly different heights; a line chart whose
    line rises gently to the right with small round dots at its turning points; a horizontal
    bar chart of FOUR bars; a pie chart divided into just TWO parts, the larger part about
    three quarters of the circle; and a five-pointed radar chart with one filled polygon
    inside it. EVERY CHART HAS NO AXIS LABELS, NO LEGEND, NO TITLE AND NO NUMBERS ON IT —
    the charts are shapes only. Use the pale tint, #317d78 and #2a6d69 for the chart shapes,
    with #c28229 used only once or twice as a single highlighted bar or dot.
  • FIVE PEOPLE WORK IN THIS ROOM, all East Asian, none of them looking at the viewer, none
    of them standing in the exact centre of the panel:
      – TWO PEOPLE STAND TOGETHER IN FRONT OF THE BIG SCREEN, seen from behind and slightly
        to the side, much smaller than the screen: a woman on the left with a short bob
        pointing with one finger at the middle of the island, and a man beside her holding
        an open paper folder against his chest, looking where she points.
      – ONE MAN SITS AT A DESK in the lower left of the panel, leaning slightly forward,
        both hands on a keyboard, looking at his own small screen.
      – ONE WOMAN WALKS ACROSS the lower right of the panel carrying a stack of papers, her
        head turned towards the big screen.
      – ONE MAN CROUCHES beside a small screen on a side desk on the right, one hand resting
        on the desk, checking it.
    Their expressions are calm, attentive and ordinary — they are doing their work. Nobody
    is alarmed, nobody is celebrating, nobody is pointing at the viewer.
  • THE SMALL SCREENS in this room (the one on the seated man's desk and the one the
    crouching man is checking) each show ONE simple shape only: one shows a single column
    chart of four bars, the other a single rising line. Nothing else on them — no code, no
    text, no icons, no logos.

THE FOUR SIDE PANELS — four different places where ordinary people are having their teeth
checked on an ordinary day. EACH PANEL HAS EXACTLY TWO PEOPLE, drawn simply and larger than
you would expect, because these panels are small. Each panel is a warm, calm, everyday
moment — nobody is frightened, nobody is in pain, no blood, no close-up of an open mouth.
  • TOP LEFT — A CLINIC RECEPTION DESK. A woman in her fifties stands at the counter handing
    a small plain card to a receptionist in a pale clinic top who reaches out to take it. On
    the wall behind them hangs one framed picture of a single tooth shape, a drawing only,
    with NO writing on it.
  • BOTTOM LEFT — A DENTAL CHAIR. A patient lies back in a dental chair with his mouth
    closed and his eyes calm; a dentist in a pale clinic top sits beside him holding a small
    round mouth mirror, leaning in to look. A simple round overhead lamp on an arm is above
    them. No blood, no drill, no tray of instruments in close-up.
  • TOP RIGHT — A COMMUNITY HALL OR SCHOOL ROOM. A folding table with two simple chairs; a
    dentist in a pale clinic top sits on one side and a teenage boy sits on the other with
    his hands on his knees, waiting his turn. Behind them, THREE very small simplified
    figures stand in a short queue, drawn as simple shapes.
  • BOTTOM RIGHT — LOOKING AT AN X-RAY. A dentist stands beside a pale lightbox on the wall
    with one hand raised towards it, explaining; a man in his thirties sits on a stool
    looking at it. The image on the lightbox is a SINGLE SIMPLE ROW OF TOOTH SHAPES in pale
    grey, with NO numbers, NO letters and NO detail.

PEOPLE — Simplified illustrative people: minimal features (small dot or short-line eyes, a
simple line mouth, a small nose), natural realistic head-to-body proportions, believable
ages, East Asian faces. Not photorealistic. Not faceless. No oversized heads, no noodle
limbs. Nobody looks at the viewer. Roughly half the people are women and half are men, of
mixed ages from teenage to sixties.
CLOTHING — clinic staff wear short-sleeved clinic tops in #b7d7d3 with their folds and
shadows in #99b8b4. Everyone else wears ordinary everyday clothes, and EVERY PERSON'S
GARMENT IS A DIFFERENT COLOUR from the people next to them — muted clay rose, mustard,
sage, soft blue, cream, warm grey. Clothes are NEVER flat single-tone shapes: model every
garment with two or three tones of its own colour, with soft coloured-pencil shading in the
folds, along the sleeves, under the collar and at the hem, and draw the real details —
collar, cuff, sleeve seam, hem, a pocket.

LIGHT AND COLOUR — Ordinary bright indoor daytime, ONE light source: clean daylight from
the left, warm and even. No long orange shadows, not sunset, not lamplight, no spotlight,
no screens lighting faces from below, no dark room. Across the upper part of the middle
panel only, above the people and not touching them, draw FOUR OR FIVE long, thin, white
hand-drawn lines running the same way at the same shallow angle, evenly spaced, solid where
they begin and thinning to dry flecks — soft chalk quality, clearly hand-made. These lines
must never begin at anyone's mouth, nose, hand or body, must never loop or curl back on
themselves, must never cross the panel dividing lines, and must never resemble smoke, steam
or breath.
Most coloured areas sit in the range HSL saturation 30–50 and lightness 70–85 — clearly
coloured and readable, never washed out into near-white pastels and never heavy or muddy.
About half the picture is chromatic. Use AT LEAST FIVE clearly different colours in each
panel and give each to a named object: the clinic tops, the patients' clothes, the walls,
the wooden desks, the potted plant, the chairs, the folders.
ACCENT — #317d78 and #2a6d69 belong to the island, the charts and the clinic tops. #c28229
appears ONLY in the four small dots, the two warning triangles and one highlighted bar. Do
NOT wash the image in either colour, do NOT tint the skin, the walls, the floor or the paper
with them, and do NOT make them vivid.

MOTION MARKS — in exactly three places, draw TWO OR THREE very short, straight, same-angle
hand-drawn strokes, no longer than a third of a palm's width, solid where they begin and
fading out: beside the pointing woman's fingertip, above the seated man's wrists as he
types, and beside the walking woman's shoulder. Nothing else. No speed lines across the
background, no radiating focus lines, no sparkles, no speech bubbles of any kind.

CRITICAL — THE ONLY CHARACTERS ANYWHERE IN THIS PICTURE ARE: the digits and percent signs
78.7% , 24.7% , 9.3% and 6.9% on the big screen, and the exclamation stroke inside the two
small amber warning triangles. THERE IS NO OTHER WRITING OF ANY KIND ANYWHERE — no place
names, no country name, no axis labels, no legends, no chart titles, no headings, no
captions, no numbers on the clock, no numbers on the charts, no words on folders, keyboards,
screens, pictures, badges, doors or clothing, in any language or alphabet. Do NOT draw rows
of short dashes, squiggles or wavy lines anywhere to imitate lines of writing. Where writing
would normally appear, leave the surface plain. There are no logos and no watermarks.

AVOID — greyscale; photorealism; a photorealistic mouth, gum or tooth; faceless or
noodle-limbed figures; thick uniform black outlines; even mechanical vector linework; a dark
room; glowing monitors; neon; chrome, metallic or iridescent gradients; glow, sparkle or
lens flare; grey, silver, white or blue-grey hair; wrinkles, cheek shadow or facial
modelling; a frightened, sad or pained face; anyone shouting, celebrating or high-fiving;
blood, wounds or red inflamed gums; decayed, dark, broken or missing teeth; a red or orange
heat map; county borders, grid lines or dotted coastlines on the island; a second island or
any other country in the picture; a world map; arrows of any kind; ticks, crosses, plus or
minus signs; speech bubbles; more than two people in any side panel; a crowd; anyone looking
at the viewer; a whole image tinted green; flat untextured single-tone clothing; a bare
empty room.
