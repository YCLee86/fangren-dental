## 案 A — 診間裡的並肩：醫師坐在旁邊，一個大泡泡把這一步講完

梗：病人剛聽到「這一顆要用另一種材料」，醫師搬了張椅子坐到他旁邊講。
上方一個大泡泡分三段，講「主通道清乾淨 → 末端還有細小的分支 → 材料流進去把它們封起來」。
右邊四分之一格是幾個月後，他在家裡的餐桌前正常吃飯。

情緒：擔心 → 被接住。支持感來自**醫師的位置**（坐在旁邊、不是隔著桌子）與攤開的手掌。
風險：結構和〈拔智齒〉那張最接近（人＋泡泡＋右側後日談），要靠場景與姿態拉開。

```
Editorial illustration, 16:9 landscape.

STYLE — READ THIS SECTION FIRST, IT MATTERS MORE THAN ANYTHING ELSE BELOW.
Contemporary printed-magazine editorial illustration, clearly hand-drawn. Linework in warm
dark brown or soft charcoal, NEVER pure black: thin, hand-drawn, the weight visibly varies
along each stroke, strokes taper and sometimes break. Colour applied like soft coloured
pencil and light marker; flat fills with two or three tones per hue; no smooth decorative
gradients — a gradient may only ever describe light. A fine even paper grain sits over
every surface of the whole image. No garment, wall or floor is a flat single-tone shape:
each one carries visible pencil texture and two or three tones of its own colour, with
shading in the folds, along the sleeves, under a collar, at a hem. Warm colours and cool
colours must both be clearly present, and every person wears a different colour. Colour
throughout — never greyscale, never monochrome, never washed out into near-white pastels.

STRUCTURE — One illustration divided into a LARGE LEFT PANEL taking about three quarters
of the width and a NARROW RIGHT PANEL taking about one quarter, separated by a single thin
hand-drawn vertical line.

LEFT PANEL — a small neighbourhood dental consulting room, late afternoon. A woman dentist
of about 40, East Asian, with a short neat bob, in pale sage scrubs, sits on a low stool
BESIDE the patient and turned towards him — not opposite him, not behind a desk, not
standing over him. She leans slightly forward; one hand rests open, palm up, on the arm of
his chair, and the other holds a small simple tooth model at chest height. Her expression
is calm, attentive and kind; she is in the middle of explaining something.
The patient is an East Asian man of about 45 sitting upright in the treatment chair, NOT
lying back. The hand nearest the viewer is still resting lightly against the cheek nearest
the viewer, though he is no longer pressing it. His eyebrows are slightly raised and his
mouth is closed in a small uncertain line — he is concerned and listening carefully, NOT
frightened, NOT in pain, NOT crying. His far shoulder has already dropped: he is beginning
to relax. Neither of them looks at the viewer.
The room must read unmistakably as a dental clinic and must not look empty: the head of a
treatment chair with its pale upholstery, a small instrument tray with three or four simple
tools laid tidily and NOT in close-up, a transparent tooth model on a low shelf, a wall
poster carrying ONLY an abstract drawing of low mountains and no writing whatsoever, a
potted plant, a stack of blank folders, a window with soft afternoon light.

THE BUBBLE — In the upper half of the left panel, ONE large rounded-rectangle thought
bubble with a hand-drawn outline, connected to the two of them by three small dots. It is
divided into THREE equal sections by two thin hand-drawn vertical lines. NO ARROWS anywhere.
The three sections are ONE progression, not three separate ideas, so all three show THE
SAME simplified single tooth root, drawn large, at the same angle and the same size:
  • section 1 — the root with its one main canal drawn as a clean pale open channel down
    the middle, freshly cleaned; near the tip of the root, three or four very fine side
    branches spread outwards like the last thin roots of a plant, and they are still dark;
  • section 2 — the same root, the same view: the main canal is still clean, but the fine
    side branches near the tip are clearly darker, each holding a few small dark specks;
  • section 3 — the same root again: a soft pale cream material has flowed down the main
    canal and out into EVERY one of those fine side branches, filling them completely to
    their ends; the dark specks are gone and a faint warm halo surrounds the filled tip.
Keep the drawing simple and illustrative — a friendly diagram, never a textbook anatomy
chart, never a photorealistic mouth interior, never a cutaway of a whole jaw. The material
is a soft rounded flow of pale cream with two tones of shading; it is NOT tooth-shaped, has
no crown, no roots of its own and no face.
KEEP THE DIAGRAM INSIDE THE BUBBLE. Nothing resembling a tooth root, a canal, fine branches
or pale filling material may appear anywhere else in the picture — not on the floor, not on
the wall, not on the poster, not on the folders.

RIGHT PANEL — a few months later, the same man at his own dining table at home in the
evening, eating a simple family dinner with his wife, chewing comfortably on the side that
was treated, his shoulders loose and his face quietly ordinary. Warm lamplight over the
table, simple home clutter: a rice bowl, a plate of greens, a soup pot, a small kettle, a
calendar on the wall carrying NO writing and NO numbers. He does not look at the viewer and
there is no medicine bag anywhere.

LIGHT — One warm light source per panel and no more: the afternoon window on the left, the
table lamp on the right. Three or four SHORT PARALLEL WHITE HAND-DRAWN ARCS of light fall
from the window across the left panel, all running the same way, evenly spaced, no longer
than one head-width, solid where they begin and thinning to dry flecks — moving light, not
an object. They must NOT be one long continuous ribbon, must NOT loop or curl back, must
NOT cross the dividing line into the right panel, and must NOT resemble smoke, steam or a
spirit leaving a body.

PEOPLE — Simplified illustrative people, all of them East Asian / Taiwanese: minimal
features (small dot or short-line eyes, a simple line mouth, a small nose), natural
realistic head-to-body proportions, believable ages. Not photorealistic. Not faceless. No
oversized heads, no noodle limbs. Nobody looks at the viewer. Expressions are natural,
gentle and appropriate to the moment — nobody grins, nobody is scared, nobody is mocking
anyone.

COLOUR — Muted and multi-coloured, every colour kept low: cream and off-white as the
ground, dusty sage, soft powder blue, warm grey, pale butter yellow, clay. The accent
colour of this article is a MUTED BRICK RED (#ae4f4d, with a deeper #89202d for its darker
tone) and it appears ONLY as small accents on objects — the fabric of the dentist's stool,
a narrow stripe along the lower wall, the frame of the poster, a mug on the shelf, the
cloth on the dining table. Never wash the whole picture in red, never a bright or neon red,
never red skin, never red light, never red gums. Teeth and the pale filling material stay
clean near-white and take none of this colour. THERE IS NO BLOOD ANYWHERE IN THIS IMAGE.

CRITICAL — NO WRITING ANYWHERE IN THE IMAGE. No text, letters, words, numbers, logos,
signage, captions, panel labels, numeral clock or calendar faces or watermarks, in any
language. The wall poster shows only the abstract mountains; folders, packaging, bottles,
tubes, the calendar, the scrubs and all clothing are blank; any screen is blank; there are
no arrows or labels anywhere, including between the three sections of the bubble. Where
writing would normally appear, leave the surface plain.

AVOID — greyscale; photorealism; a photorealistic mouth interior; a dense close-up of
dental instruments, syringes, needles or drills; blood; pain, fear or tears; a frightened
or crying patient; a patient lying flat with the dentist looming over him; anyone looking
at the viewer; faceless or noodle-limbed figures; oversized heads; thick uniform black
outlines; chrome or iridescent gradients; decayed, dark, broken or missing teeth; the brick
```

## 案 B — 同一張椅子，三次回診：只有一件事在變

梗：三格用**同一個機位**（同一張診療椅、同一個角度、同一扇窗），只有時間與他的姿態在變。
① 初診：他一手指著自己的牙齦（那裡長了個小腫包），眉頭皺著；醫師坐在旁邊前傾聽著。
② 治療後幾週：兩人一起對著窗光看一張片子，圈裡是根尖那些細分支被填滿。
③ 幾個月後：他坐得鬆，醫師把小模型收回托盤，窗外的光更亮。

情緒：靠「同一個位置」讀出時間，支持感來自**醫師三格都在同一個地方**。
規格出處：第七節第 2 條（同一機位重複是最好讀的手法）、第 10 條（放大圈才有足夠像素）。
風險：三格容易被畫成三個不同房間，所以提示詞裡把「機位不准動」寫得很重。

## 案 C — 那一夜，顯微鏡底下（人不是主角，場景是主角）

梗：夜裡的診間，一盞顯微鏡的燈是整張圖唯一的光。醫師伏在顯微鏡前，助理在旁邊遞東西，
病人躺著、眼睛閉著但眉毛是鬆的。畫面上方一個小圓圈是顯微鏡裡看到的世界：
根尖那些細小的分支被淡色材料一一填滿。窗外是斗六夜裡的街。

情緒：安靜的專注。支持感不是靠表情，是靠**「你睡著的時候有人在替你守著看不見的地方」**。
規格出處：第三節「一個大場景塞很多小事」＋「整張只有一個光源，暖色」＋「人物不超過畫面一半」。
風險：夜景容易被 high-key 洗白（第七節第 18 條踩過），所以提示詞裡明講房間要暗、牆不可以留白。
